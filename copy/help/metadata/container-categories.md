# Container Categories

Select one or more categories that best describe your container's functionality:

- Categories help users discover and understand your container
- Choose all categories that apply to your tool
- At least one category is required

Use **arterial spin labelling** for ASL perfusion imaging tools. It can accompany quantitative imaging or workflows when those categories also apply.

Use **preclinical imaging** for small-animal, Bruker, microPET/microCT, phantom, and non-rodent preclinical tools (`rodent imaging` remains available for backwards compatibility).

Use **molecular imaging** for PET, SPECT, nuclear medicine, and tracer kinetic modeling tools.
