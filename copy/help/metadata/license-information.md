# License Information

Specify licenses for your container and any included software:

**Options:**
- **SPDX Licenses:** Choose a standard license, or enter an SPDX expression such as `MIT OR Apache-2.0`. `LicenseRef-` identifiers, `NOASSERTION` and `NONE` also need a URL.
- **Custom License:** Enter the license title and the URL of its terms. Both are required.

This is important for legal compliance and distribution.
